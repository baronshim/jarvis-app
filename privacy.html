Jarvis privacy policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Jarvis is a personal assistant used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer. It has no other users.

What Google data Jarvis uses

  • Gmail (read): to search and read the owner's email when the owner asks a question about it. Reading never marks mail as read or changes it.
  • Gmail (compose): only to save a draft email after the owner approves that specific draft. Jarvis never sends email; the owner sends drafts from Gmail.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): to answer questions about the owner's schedule.
  • Google Drive (read-only): to find and read the owner's files when asked.

Where data goes

  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, in files only the owner's account can read.
  • Google data is fetched only when the owner asks for it and is used only to answer the owner. It is processed by the AI model the owner uses (Anthropic's Claude) to produce the answer.
  • Jarvis does not sell, share, or transfer Google data to anyone else, does not use it for advertising, and does not use it to train AI models.

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Jarvis's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections and by deleting the local sign-in files.
